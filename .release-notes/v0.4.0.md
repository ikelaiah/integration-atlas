Historical graph comparison is now available in Scan history.

- Applied scans save redacted before and after graph checkpoints. Preview scans leave no history.
- Compare any two ordered checkpoints in the same workspace to see net entity and relationship additions, changes and retirements.
- Filter and search the change list, then expand a row to inspect before and after field values. Counts cover the complete comparison; details are paged.
- Existing scans retain their per-scan diffs. Their full past graph cannot be reconstructed, so they are not offered as checkpoints. Your first v0.4.0 scan captures its own before state.
- The [comparison guide](https://ikelaiah.github.io/integration-atlas/historical-comparison.html) explains the workflow. Comparison stays local and requires no scanning agent.
